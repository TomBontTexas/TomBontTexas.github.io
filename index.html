🎮 Texas Bont's Web Apps

Browser-based gaming aids and games. No installs, no accounts — everything runs on your device.

None of these are ever really "finished" — they're hobby projects I work on whenever time permits, or whenever a new idea strikes. Use them as much as you like, just know that if you don't save your own work, it may well be gone the next time something changes.

GASCAR

GASCAR Race Control

Offline companion app for the tabletop racing game Warp Space: GASCAR — build ships and crews, design racecourses, and run races.

 WSVB

Warp Space Vehicle Builder

Design and manage starships and vehicles for the Warp Space tabletop RPG.

 WSHB

Warp Space Hero Builder

Build heroes and characters for the Warp Space tabletop RPG.

 RLCB

Rivers of London Investigator Sheet

Interactive character sheet for the Rivers of London RPG.

 HP-28S

HP-28S 3D Adventure

A browser remake of Paul Dale's 1990 HP-28S dungeon game.

 ZENER

Zener Card Test

Test your ESP with the classic Zener deck: guess all 25 cards blind, then see your score and track your history.
All of these apps run entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
